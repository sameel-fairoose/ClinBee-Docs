SMS Communication Policy

This page explains how SMS communications are used by businesses that utilize the Clinbee platform.

About Clinbee

Clinbee is a communication platform that helps clinics and service businesses respond to customers who contact them by phone. The platform may send automated SMS messages when a call is missed so that businesses can assist customers quickly.

How SMS Messages Are Triggered

Customers or patients (both new and existing) may contact a clinic or business by calling the publicly listed phone number of that business.

If the call is missed or staff are unavailable to answer the phone, the Clinbee platform may automatically send a follow-up SMS message to the caller to help continue the conversation and provide assistance.

Purpose of SMS Messages

SMS messages may include:

  • Responses to missed calls
  • Appointment assistance
  • Clinic hours and location information
  • Customer support responses
  • General service inquiries
  • Clinics specific FAQs

Consent to Receive Messages

Messages are only sent to individuals who initiate contact with a clinic or business by calling their phone number. By contacting the business by phone, the caller may receive a follow-up SMS related to their inquiry.

Opt-Out Instructions

You can stop receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message.

After you send the STOP message, you will no longer receive SMS messages from that business.

Help

If you need assistance, reply HELP to any message or contact the business directly.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and service plan.
